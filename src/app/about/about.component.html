I currently work at Reveleer as a Senior Software Engineer. Much of my work at Reveleer is dedicated to architectural strategy or day-to-day tech lead responsibilities. For a detailed breakdown of my experience, give my resume a look.

Previously I worked at Driveway.com creating backend services for shopping Driveway's new and pre-owned vehicle inventory, primarily using Kotlin. The majority of my work at Driveway revolved around inventory search which was a great opportunity for me to learn about the very scientific yet subjective world of search. I greatly enjoyed learning GraphQL and I am continually impressed by its potential for performance benefits.

Prior to my time with Driveway I worked at EVRAZ North America as a Full Stack Software Developer. At EVRAZ I maintained decades-old monolithic apps written in Java and C, and helped implement new Javascript web apps. I graduated from the University of Portland in May 2017 with a B.S. in Computer Science and a minor in Mathematics.

For me, coding is more than just a job. In my spare time I maintain an open source Discord bot that runs ready-checks for folks playing online games which require group coordination and is currently used on 700+ servers. The bot has been in relatively steady development since late 2017, and a few years ago I upgraded the bot to use Discord's shiny new (at the time) slash command interface. Most recently the bot got a glow up of its own when I rewrote it in Kotlin and Spring. I have a more full explanation of the rewrite of the bot in the github repo for the bot if that interests you.

When I'm not coding I like to play music and I've been playing for the last 23 years or so. I started with piano, then moved on briefly to cello, tried my hand at the trumpet, then stuck to euphonium for about 10 years. Along the way I picked up guitar, bass, drums, and singing. I occasionally record covers of songs as the mood strikes me. One of my favorite projects was Irving Berlin's "White Christmas" which I got partway through covering before my computer died and lost the project file. You can listen to what remains of the song here (check your volume before clicking!). At the moment, my work-in-progress tune is Archie, Marry Me by Alvvays which has been quite a fun recording challenge, and I've been working on my ability to play the incredibly challenging Chet Atkins version of Scott Joplin's "The Entertainer".
